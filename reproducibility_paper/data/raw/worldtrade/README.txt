Data from paper: Gaulier, G. & Zignago, S. Baci: International trade database at the product-level. the 1994-2007 version.470
Working Papers 2010-23, CEPII (2010).

Specifically downloaded from their online database: https://cepii.fr/ (Data --> BACI --> HS02).

Each dataset (BACI_HS02_YYYY_V202401b.csv where YYYY is the year from 2002 to 2022) contains: t (year), i (exporter), j (importer), k (product), v (value, in thousands USD), q (quantity in metric tones).

We only consider even years.

The conversion between ids and names for countries and products is found in {codes,product}_codes_V202401b.csv
