These are a reduced version of the adjacency matrices of the connectomes from: Witvliet, D. et al. Connectomes across development reveal principles of brain maturation. Nature 596, 257449 – 261, 10.1038/s41586-021-03778-8 (2021).

They were processed following reproducibility_paper/data_processing_celegans.ipynb (we took the first 60 neurons of the connectomes).
